# How to get your catalog live on Facebook

Follow these clicks inside Catalog Forge — from your Shopify store to a live Facebook catalog. We’ll use Gibun as the example, but it works for any Shopify store.

## 1. Connect your store

1. Open the home page.
2. You’ll see a box called **Connect Shopify Store**. It already says `store.gibun.at`.
3. Click **Preview Products**.
4. You’ll see “31 products found.” Those are your teas ready for Facebook. Workshops are already left out.

## 2. Check for problems

1. Click **Validate** at the top of the page.
2. Click **Lint Feed**.
3. If everything is green, you’re good.
4. If you see red, it tells you what to fix in Shopify. For Gibun it flags 1 refill bag with no photo — just add a photo to that product in Shopify and check again.

## 3. Make your products look like ads

1. Click **Open HTML Editor** at the top.
2. Your tea appears in the middle — photo in a soft frame, name underneath, black pill with the price.
3. **Move things:** Click the title or the price pill and drag it. Use the little corner handles to make it bigger or smaller.
4. **Change the look:** On the right you can change colour, size and font. If you type `{{price}}` it will show the real price for each tea.
5. **Try the assistant:** At the top type something like “add red sale badge” and click **Generate**.
6. **See every placement:** At the top switch from `1:1` (feed) to `9:16` (Stories). Click **All sizes** — your one design automatically fits every placement. The product photo stretches and the title/price stay nicely at the bottom.

## 4. Save your design

1. Click **Save to Server** at the top. It changes to **Saved ✓**.
2. A purple bar appears that says **Enriched Feed**. That is your new catalog link — it has your design on every product.

## 5. Put it on Facebook

1. In that purple bar click **Copy Enriched Feed URL**.
2. Go to Facebook → **Commerce Manager** → **Create Catalog** and name it (e.g. “Gibun”).
3. Go to **Data Sources → Add Items → Data Feed → Enter URL** and paste the link you just copied.
4. Set it to **Daily** and click **Fetch**.
5. After a minute you’ll see all your teas with your new design. Create a **Product Set** (like “All Teas”) and start an **Advantage+ Catalog** campaign — Facebook will now show your branded pictures instead of plain packshots.

That’s it: **Paste → Check → Design → Save → Copy → Paste into Facebook.**

Tip: You can see the whole journey from store to live ads on the **Story Map** page.
